Supplementary Material for the paper 
MARVEL analysis of high-resolution rovibrational spectra
of the 18O12C18O, 17O12C18O, and 18O13C18O isotopologues of carbon dioxide
by Ala’a A. A. Azzam, Sumaya A. A. Azzam, Karam A. A. Aburummana, Jonathan Tennyson, Sergei N.
Yurchenko, Attila G. Cs ́asz ́ar, Tibor Furtenbacher 

The Supplementary Material contains the following files:
EnergyLevels_728.txt
EnergyLevels_828.txt
EnergyLevels_838.txt
Transitions_728.txt
Transitions_828.txt
Transitions_838.txt

The EnergyLevels files contains the 
empirical (MARVEL) energy levels in the following format:
quantum numbers, (J v1 v2 l2 v3 r p)
energy level (in cm-1), 
uncertainty (in  cm-1),
nbTr: number of transitions which determine the given empirical rovibrational energy

The Transitions files contains 
all the transitions data, utilized during the MARVEL analysis,
according to the following format:
transition wavenumber (in cm-1, deleted lines have negative wavenumber entry),
initial uncertainty (in cm-1),
final MARVEL uncertainty (in cm-1),
quantum numbers (upper state, lower state, see the EnergyLevels files),
tag.linenumber
